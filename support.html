Groundfall — Support

Groundfall is a running journal built on Apple Health — routes, entries, and achievements drawn from your real running history.

Need help?

Email ryanerenteria@gmail.com with any questions, bug reports, or feedback, and I'll get back to you.

Common questions

Why is Rituals/Journal/Seals empty?
Groundfall reads your running workouts from Apple Health. If you don't have any recorded runs in the Health app yet, there's nothing for Groundfall to show. Once you log a run (via Apple Watch, a GPS running app, or manual entry in Health), it'll appear.

Do I need Strava?
No — Strava is optional. Connecting it in Settings unlocks a few additional achievements, but every core feature works fully without it.

What does the one-time unlock include?
It unlocks the Seals page in full and every Journal entry beyond the 3 most recent. Rituals, route viewing, and sharing to Instagram are free regardless.

I paid but it's not unlocked — help?
Try "Restore Purchases," found in Settings and on the unlock screen. If that doesn't resolve it, email the address above.
